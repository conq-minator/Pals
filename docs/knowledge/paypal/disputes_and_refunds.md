# PayPal Disputes, Holds, and Refund Timelines

## 1. Why Payments Go on Hold
PayPal places temporary holds on incoming funds under several circumstances:
- **New Seller Account:** Accounts with limited selling history undergo a 21-day probationary hold.
- **Unusual Transaction Spike:** Sudden spikes in volume, order value, or selling categories.
- **Disputed Transaction:** When a buyer files an Item Not Received (INR) or Significantly Not As Described (SNAD) dispute.

## 2. Refund Processing Times
- **PayPal Balance:** Refund is credited instantly.
- **Credit / Debit Cards:** Refund reaches the issuing bank within 3 to 5 business days, depending on the card issuer's billing cycle (can take up to 30 days on certain international debit cards).
- **Bank Account:** Takes 3 to 5 business days to clear back to the user's bank account.

## 3. Resolving Limitations & Releasing Holds
Merchants can release holds faster by providing verified carrier tracking numbers (funds release 24 hours after verified delivery confirmation) or obtaining buyer confirmation.
