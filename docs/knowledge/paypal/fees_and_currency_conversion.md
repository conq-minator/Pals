# PayPal Fee Structure & Currency Conversion Guide

## 1. Domestic & Consumer Transactions
- **Purchases:** Free for buyers when making purchases online through PayPal checkout in domestic currency.
- **Funding Sources:** No surcharge for using linked bank accounts, PayPal balance, or debit cards. Standard card issuer fees may apply if funding via international credit cards.

## 2. Cross-Border & International Commercial Payments
- **Standard International Receiving Fee:** 4.4% + fixed fee based on currency (e.g., $0.30 USD).
- **Domestic Merchant Rates:** Typically 2.5% to 3.5% + fixed fee.

## 3. Currency Conversion Rates & Spread
- When a payment requires a currency conversion, PayPal applies a retail exchange rate that includes a currency conversion spread (typically 3.0% to 4.0% above the wholesale exchange rate obtained from an external financial institution).
- Users can choose during checkout whether to let PayPal perform the conversion or let their card issuer convert the currency.
