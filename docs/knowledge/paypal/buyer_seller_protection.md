# PayPal Buyer and Seller Protection Policy Reference

## 1. PayPal Buyer Protection
PayPal Buyer Protection reimburses buyers if an eligible order either does not arrive or significantly differs from the seller's description.

### Key Eligibility Criteria
- The purchase must be made using a PayPal transaction.
- The buyer must report the issue within 180 days of the transaction date.
- The buyer's account must remain in good standing.
- Qualifying items: Most physical goods, including electronics, computers, clothing, and books.
- Excluded items: Real estate, motorized vehicles, industrial machinery, custom-made items, and gift cards.

### Dispute Process Steps
1. **Open a Dispute:** Contact the seller via PayPal Resolution Center within 180 days.
2. **Negotiation Window:** Both parties have 20 days to resolve the matter directly.
3. **Escalate to a Claim:** If no agreement is reached, either party can escalate to a PayPal Claim. PayPal then reviews evidence (tracking numbers, receipts) and issues a binding decision.

## 2. PayPal Seller Protection
Protects merchants against unauthorized payments and claims alleging an item was not received.

### Requirements for Sellers
- Ship to the verified address listed on the Transaction Details page.
- Retain valid online tracking information or proof of delivery with signature confirmation for high-value orders ($750 / ₹60,000+).
- Respond to inquiries within the requested timeframe (usually 10 days).
