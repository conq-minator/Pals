# PayPal account, login and security

A summary for PayPal users, written for PALS from PayPal's public help. For anything specific to your account, log in to PayPal or contact PayPal through its Help Center.

## Opening an account

- Sign up at paypal.com or in the PayPal app with your email address or phone number and a password.
- Choose a **personal account** for shopping and sending money, or a **business account** to accept payments as a seller.
- Confirm your email address and phone number, then link a bank account or card so you can pay.

## Can't log in

- Choose **Forgot password?** (or **Having trouble logging in?**) on the login page and follow the steps to reset it by email or text.
- If your phone number changed, use the other options offered on the login page, or contact PayPal through its Help Center.
- Never use a "PayPal support" phone number or link from an email, text or search ad you weren't expecting; go to paypal.com yourself.

## Keeping your account safe

- Turn on **two-step verification** in your security settings, so a code is needed as well as your password.
- PayPal will never ask you for your password, full card number, bank login or one-time codes by email, text, phone or chat.
- Check that the address bar shows paypal.com before you log in.
- Forward suspicious messages that claim to be from PayPal to PayPal, then delete them.
- If you think someone else used your account: change your password, review recent activity, and report any payments you didn't make in the Resolution Center.

## Account limitations

PayPal may limit an account, which restricts what you can do (for example sending or withdrawing money) until a problem is fixed. Common reasons are unusual activity, a security concern, or PayPal needing to confirm information about you.

To lift a limitation, log in and open the notification about it (in the Resolution Center or the bell icon). PayPal lists what it needs, such as confirming your identity, your address, or a bank account, or providing documents. Once you have provided everything and PayPal has reviewed it, the limitation can be removed.

## Closing your account

You can close a personal account from your account settings. Before closing, withdraw any balance and make sure no disputes or payments are still open.

## Your privacy

PayPal's Privacy Statement explains what personal information PayPal collects, how it uses and shares it, how long it keeps it, and your rights over it.
