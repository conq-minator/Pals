# What PALS is

PALS (Personal AI Life & Shopping Companions) is a shopping and payment assistant built on PayPal. You chat with **Pali**, the assistant in the app. Behind Pali, four agents work together. Each one has one job and none of them can do another's job:

- **GLY** is the only agent you talk to. It understands your message, answers questions about PayPal and about this app, and passes shopping and payment work to the other agents.
- **ARB** finds products, compares merchants, checks prices and coupons, and prepares offers for you to approve.
- **DEFENDER** is the security check. It inspects every payment before any money moves and can block it.
- **PULSE** is the only agent that talks to PayPal. It creates the PayPal order, takes the payment after you approve it on PayPal, and keeps the record of your PALS payments.

## What you can ask Pali

- **Questions about PayPal**: fees, buyer protection, disputes, refunds, privacy, how PayPal checkout and the PayPal APIs work. Pali answers from PayPal's official documentation and shows the sources it used.
- **Questions about this app**: how deals are ranked, what the trust score means, how approval and the security check work, why a payment was stopped.
- **Shopping**: "find wireless earbuds under 100 dollars", "compare laptops for programming under 1200". Results arrive in the chat when ARB finishes; you can keep chatting meanwhile.
- **Getting an item ready**: "get the first one ready" or name the item. ARB builds an offer with the final price and any coupon.
- **Your payments**: "show my transactions" lists the payments you made through PALS.

## What Pali will not do

- Pali never pays on its own. Every payment needs you to press Approve in the app and then confirm on PayPal's own page.
- Pali cannot see or change your PayPal account, your balance, or payments you made outside PALS.
- Pali cannot issue refunds or open disputes for you. For a refund, contact the merchant first; if that fails, you can open a dispute in PayPal's Resolution Center.
- Pali gives information, not legal or financial advice.
- PALS never sees your PayPal password or card details. You enter those only on PayPal's site.

## How to use it, step by step

1. Ask for what you want, with a budget if you have one: "find running shoes under 80 dollars".
2. Read the results. Each one shows the merchant, the full price and why it was picked.
3. Say which one you want ("get the second one ready"). An offer card appears with the final price.
4. Press Approve. DEFENDER checks the payment; if it passes, a **Pay with PayPal** button appears.
5. Pay on PayPal's page. When PayPal sends you back, PULSE completes the payment and a receipt appears in the chat.
