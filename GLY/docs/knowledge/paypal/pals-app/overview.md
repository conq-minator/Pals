# What PALS is

PALS (Personal AI Life & Shopping Companions) is a shopping and payment assistant built on PayPal. You chat with the PALS assistant. Behind the chat, four parts of PALS work together, each with one job:

- **The assistant** is the only part you talk to. It understands your message, answers questions about PayPal and about this app, and passes shopping and payment work to the other parts.
- **Deal search** finds products, compares merchants, checks prices and coupons, and prepares offers for you to approve.
- **The security check** inspects payments. It runs before any money moves and can block a payment.
- **Payments** is the only part that talks to PayPal. It creates the PayPal order, takes the payment after you approve it on PayPal, and keeps the record of your PALS payments.

## What you can ask PALS

- **Questions about PayPal**: fees, buyer protection, disputes, refunds, privacy, how PayPal checkout and the PayPal APIs work. PALS answers from PayPal's official documentation and shows the sources it used.
- **Questions about this app**: how deals are ranked, what the trust score means, how approval and the security check work, why a payment was stopped.
- **Shopping**: "find wireless earbuds under 100 dollars", "compare laptops for programming under 1200". Results arrive in the chat when PALS finishes; you can keep chatting meanwhile.
- **Getting an item ready**: "get the first one ready" or name the item. PALS builds an offer with the final price and any coupon.
- **Your payments**: "show my transactions" lists the payments you made through PALS.

## What PALS will not do

- PALS never pays on its own. Every payment needs you to press Approve in the app and then confirm on PayPal's own page.
- PALS cannot see or change your PayPal account, your balance, or payments you made outside PALS.
- PALS cannot issue refunds or open disputes for you. For a refund, contact the merchant first; if that fails, you can open a dispute in PayPal's Resolution Center.
- PALS gives information, not legal or financial advice.
- PALS never sees your PayPal password or card details. You enter those only on PayPal's site.

## How to use it, step by step

1. Ask for what you want, with a budget if you have one: "find running shoes under 80 dollars".
2. Read the results. Each one shows the merchant, the full price and why it was picked.
3. Press **Get this ready** on the one you want (or type "get the second one ready"). Your offer appears in the offer panel with the final price.
4. Press **Approve**. PALS runs a security check; if it passes, a **Pay with PayPal** button appears in the offer panel.
5. Pay on PayPal's page. When PayPal sends you back, PALS completes the payment and shows the receipt.
