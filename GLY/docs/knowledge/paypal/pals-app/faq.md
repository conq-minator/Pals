# Common questions about PALS

## Why didn't Pali find anything?

ARB only shows items that match what you asked for and fit your budget, from merchants with a high enough trust score. Try a higher budget, a broader description ("headphones" instead of an exact model), or no merchant restriction. Live search can also be temporarily unavailable.

## Why is the price higher than the one I saw on the shop's site?

ARB shows the landed cost: the item plus shipping, fees, taxes and any currency conversion. The shop's headline price often leaves these out.

## Why was my offer in dollars when the shop is in India?

PayPal cannot charge Indian rupees, so ARB re-prices the same item from the same shop in US dollars and tells you before you approve.

## My offer expired. What now?

Offers last 15 minutes so the price is still correct when you pay. Ask Pali to get the item ready again.

## Why was my payment blocked or held?

DEFENDER found a problem, for example the amount changed, the merchant's site looks unsafe, or there were too many payment attempts in a minute. Nothing was charged. Pali's message gives the reason.

## Was I charged?

You are only charged after you confirm on PayPal's page and a receipt appears in the chat. If you see no receipt, nothing was taken. "Show my transactions" lists your PALS payments.

## Can Pali buy things without asking me?

No. Every payment needs your Approve in the app and your confirmation on PayPal's page.

## Can I get a refund through Pali?

No. Contact the merchant. If the item didn't arrive or isn't as described, open a dispute in PayPal's Resolution Center.

## Can Pali see my PayPal balance or history?

No. Pali only knows the payments you made through PALS.

## Is my password or card stored?

No. You only ever enter them on PayPal's own site.

## How does Pali answer PayPal questions?

From PayPal's official documentation stored in the app. Pali shows the sources it used. If the documents don't cover a question, Pali says so instead of guessing and points you to PayPal Help.
